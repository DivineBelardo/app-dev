**Crash Landing on You**
#A popular South Korean romantic drama that aired in 2019. It tells the story of Yoon Se-ri, a wealthy South Korean heiress who accidentally paraglides into North Korea after a storm. There, she meets Ri Jeong-hyeok, a kind and disciplined North Korean army officer who decides to protect her and help her return home. Despite the political and cultural divide between them, their bond deepens into a heartfelt and forbidden love. The drama beautifully captures themes of destiny, sacrifice, and the power of love to transcend borders.
